Your street's board, even in an outage

Needs, offers, road news and who's safe, for the few streets around you. When the network is down, posts pass from phone to phone: by a link shared over AirDrop, Nearby Share or Bluetooth, or over a mesh radio. When any phone gets back online, it brings what it's carrying, and everyone's board catches up.

Which board?

The board for where you are covers about 5 km around you. Or use a name your street agrees on.

On the board

Phone to phone

A link with the whole board inside it. Share it by AirDrop, Nearby Share, Bluetooth or a text: none of those need the internet. Whoever opens it gets every post, and passes it on in turn.

Over a mesh radio

With a Meshtastic or MeshCore radio, each new post goes out over the air, and posts from radios around you come in, hopping from radio to radio for miles. Radios cut a post to fit one packet.
