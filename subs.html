Every charge that comes back

Drop in a few months of your bank or card statement. Every charge that repeats, weekly, monthly or yearly, is found, with what it costs you a year, which ones went up, which overlap, what's due next, and a letter to cancel each one. It's all worked out on this device: the statement isn't sent anywhere. For the bills that change every month, there's Bill Watch.

Most banks have "Download transactions" or "Export" as CSV. Three months or more finds the monthly ones; a year finds the yearly ones too.
