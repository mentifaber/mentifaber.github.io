MentiPlayer

    About MentiPlayer

    One player for media that is public domain, openly licensed, or that its owners stream for free. Search runs through a small gateway on mentifaber.org that asks each source for you, so nothing here needs an account or a key.

    Where it comes from

    Internet Archive (films, classic TV, cartoons, concerts, old-time radio, audiobooks) · Library of Congress · TVmaze (TV details, CC BY-SA) · Openverse · ccMixter · Audius · Apple Music (30-second song previews, every artist) · Jamendo · Wikimedia Commons · NASA Image and Video Library · Radio Browser · SomaFM · Apple Podcasts directory and each show's public feed · Mixcloud · YouTube, Dailymotion and Sepia Search (PeerTube), shown in their own players · live news channels, in their own YouTube players · film scores from Rotten Tomatoes and IMDb, through OMDb.

    Terms

    Safe mode is on unless you turn it off: it keeps to curated sources and hides anything adult or suggestive, but no filter is perfect, so tell me about anything that slips through. Personal, non-commercial use. Everything stays the property of its owners and is played straight from them; MentiPlayer stores nothing but your saved list and place-keeping on this device. Respect each item's licence and credit. Shows that live on another host (YouTube, Dailymotion, Mixcloud, the Archive) play in that host's own player, so they stay with their owners; current network shows are details plus whatever free episodes exist. No warranty.
