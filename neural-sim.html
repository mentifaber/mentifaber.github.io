Three passes

The same question, rebuilt

Three versions of the same simulation, each a different answer to what should be visible. The earlier versions were kept rather than overwritten, because the thing being studied is how the model changes — and a replaced file destroys exactly that record.

The work

What is actually running

A network modelling emotional state rather than classification: nodes carrying valence, propagating it, settling into configurations that were not specified in advance.

The emotional network

Valence propagates and settles. Whether that resembles emotion or merely borrows its vocabulary is the open question.

The protocol

A Universal Consciousness Protocol — less a claim about consciousness than a specification for what evidence would have to look like.

Captured states

Step 0, step 50, step 100 — frames pulled from the run rather than described afterward. Prose written after the fact invents a tidier path than the one that happened.

Versions kept

v1 through v3 retained. The diff between them is the actual finding.

The three versions

Each one still runs

Open any of them — the earlier files are the real earlier files, not reconstructions.

v1 — two networks

A biological network in six regions beside a layered artificial one with dead units and dropout. Inject a stimulus, or glitch the AI and watch coherence fall.

v2 — four networks

Adds a psilocybin brain with periodic whole-network waves and a consistent AI beside the inconsistent one — then merges all four into quadrants.

v3 — mechanisms

Inhibitory neurons, theta and alpha oscillators, a thalamic gate that opens under psilocybin, gradient vanishing and mode collapse in the bad AI, residual paths and layer norm in the good one.

Cross-talk

In v3's merged view, dashed links carry bio→AI feedback and an entropy channel from the psilocybin brain into the inconsistent AI.

The honest part

What this does not show

None of it demonstrates consciousness. A system built to produce something that looks like inner state will produce something that looks like inner state — that is the build working, not evidence of anything past the build. The work is worth keeping anyway, for the same reason the word Verel had to exist: the observation is real even when the conclusion is unavailable.

simulation · not demonstration

A simulation of a thing is evidence about the simulation. Everything past that is a claim, and claims are labelled as claims.
