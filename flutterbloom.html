A living 3D garden

A real sky follows her clock from dawn to starlight, with sunlight, soft shadows, wind in the grass and a moon at night. Every helper plants real flowers, so the meadow fills in as she plays.

Build it her way

Thirteen things to build, from stepping stones and a bench to a pond full of koi, a fairy-lit tree swing, a gazebo and a Moon Gate. Each one appears in the world and stays forever.

The Butterfly Journal

Sixteen real species fly in and land on her flowers, from Monarchs and Morphos to night-only moths and a Birdwing almost no one sees. Once caught they stay, living in the garden and lending her butterfly their wings.

A garden with weather

Soft rain makes everything grow twice as fast, then a rainbow triples every catch. A bunny hops through by day, three little wishes wait every morning, the seasons bring their own surprises, and every photo she takes goes into the garden album.
