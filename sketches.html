Small builds that were finished the day they were started. They are listed here as sketches so they stop reading as unfinished obligations — which is the failure mode of keeping them in a folder called "misc."

Why they stay

Kept as sketches

Because a portfolio that pretends every afternoon was a product is lying, and because these were fun — a sufficient reason that does not require a roadmap attached to it. They are not unfinished. They are done.
