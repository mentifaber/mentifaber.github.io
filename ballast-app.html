What Ballast Is

Ballast is the weight a ship carries low in its hull. It isn't cargo and it doesn't earn anything — it's there so rough water can't turn the vessel over. Take it out to travel lighter and the first real wave capsizes you.

Money works the same way. The buffer you hold back, the bills you know are coming, the margin between your balance and zero — that's ballast, and this app exists to measure it honestly rather than flatter you. Three questions, answered continuously: what am I carrying, what's coming, and how much room do I have before I'm in trouble.

How This Works

Dashboard — live overview plus a 90-day projection showing the lowest point your balance is expected to hit, and when.

Income — recurring rules (the fast way) plus optional real paycheck logs. If you log real paychecks, those override the rule estimate.

Bills — the source of truth for every bill. Monthly bills use a day of the month; weekly and biweekly use a day of the week; one-time bills use a real date and disappear after it passes.

Calendar — bills and paydays on a real month grid, with recurrence handled properly.

Transactions — set a starting balance once, then log spending (or paste a bank CSV). Running balance and current balance calculate automatically.

Daily Spend — turns your weekly surplus into one honest "safe to spend today" number, with a buffer and split you fully control.

Analysis — category breakdown, a six-month trend, and budget vs. actual.

Keyboard: press 1–8 to jump between tabs, arrow keys to move along the tab bar, and Esc to close the setup guide.

Data & Privacy

Everything you enter is saved only in this browser, using local storage. Nothing is transmitted anywhere — there is no server, no account, and no analytics on this page. Nobody but you can see any of it.

The trade-off: your data is tied to this browser on this device. It survives refreshes and closing the tab, but it won't follow you to another browser or computer, and clearing site data will erase it. Export a backup regularly if the data matters to you — that's the only copy that outlives this browser profile.

Connecting a Real Bank Feed

A page like this cannot connect directly to your bank, and no version of it safely could — that's not a shortcoming of this build, it's how bank-data providers work.

Why not: Plaid Link and every comparable aggregator require a backend server holding a secret API key. That key can never ship inside a webpage — anyone could open dev tools, take it, and pull data from every account it touches.

Realistic options: use a consumer app that already has that infrastructure (Copilot, Monarch, YNAB, Rocket Money); build a real backend with a server, database and auth; or keep using the CSV import on the Transactions tab, which needs nothing beyond what your bank already offers.
