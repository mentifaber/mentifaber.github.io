Flutterbloom accounts

Her login is the main account. Extra accounts (like a developer account) open the same garden app but each keeps its own garden, photos and reminder, so nothing you do there touches hers.

1 · Confirm the main login

To share the garden's key with a new account, sign in once with her Flutterbloom name and password. Nothing here changes her garden.

2 · Add an account

Pick a name and password for the new account. To sign in, type the name exactly as you set it here.

Extra accounts

Sign in to an extra account in a different browser or a private window. Each browser keeps one account's garden, so the lock screen won't mix them.
