the idea

Orbit, drawn

what it is

A 3D orbital cartograph — satellites, tracked debris, and the ground tracks they leave, plotted on a live-rotating globe rather than a flat 2D pass predictor. Sibling project to Orbit Watch: where Orbit Watch is the single-satellite pass tracker, Cartograph is the wide view — everything, at once, in one scene.

how it works

Public TLE (two-line element) data feeds an SGP4 propagator to get real position vectors, rendered as a 3D scene the browser draws directly — no plugin, no native app. Objects are colored and filtered by class (active satellite, debris, station), and the camera orbits freely around the globe.

status

Pre-alpha. The render above is a placeholder — real TLE ingest and the SGP4 propagation loop are still being wired up. Treat this page as a marker for where the finished system will live, not a demo of it yet.
