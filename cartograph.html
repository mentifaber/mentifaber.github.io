the idea

Orbit, drawn

what it is

A 3D cartograph of everything, at every scale — 109 real stars to 50,000 light-years plotted from true RA/Dec/distance; 50 solar system bodies including every planet, major dwarf planet, and 3I/ATLAS (the 2025 interstellar visitor); 27 confirmed exoplanets with JWST atmospheric data; 15+ black holes from stellar-mass to ultramassive; 20+ nebulae; 20+ clusters; 14 pulsars and neutron stars; 20+ galaxies; and deep-universe structure to the edge of the observable cosmos. Every object carries 8 instrument-cited data fields sourced from Hubble, JWST, Gaia DR3, EHT, Chandra, NICER, and LIGO. Sibling project to Orbit Watch — where Orbit Watch tracks a single satellite in real time, Cartograph is the wide view.

how it works

Ten modes, one renderer. Sol Sys places each planet by true orbital radius with Kepler mechanics; Stellar converts real RA/Dec/parallax into 3D positions for 109 stars; Exoplanets groups 27 confirmed worlds with JWST and Hubble observation notes; Remnants, Nebulae, Clusters, Pulsars, Galaxy, and Deep Universe each render their category at representative scale. Tap any object for its full data panel — spectral type, temperature, mass, luminosity, instrument source, and observation year. Three.js r134 with UnrealBloom post-processing, 10,000 statistically-classified background stars, GLSL twinkling shader. No plugin, no native app.

status

Live at mentifaber.github.io/Cartograph. All modes interactive — drag to orbit, pinch or scroll to zoom, tap any object for its data panel. Data sourced from Gaia DR3 · Hubble/HST · JWST · Chandra · NICER · EHT · LIGO/Virgo · VLT ESPRESSO. Next: SGP4 propagation for a live-satellite layer, shared with Orbit Watch.
