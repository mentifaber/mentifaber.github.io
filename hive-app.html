Welcome to AutoFaber

  1. Ask anything. Type below, or press / for commands. It plans its own lookups and cites what it read.
  2. Turn on a power. Search for the live web, Council for several minds at once, Imagine for pictures, + to show it a photo, video or file.
  3. Talk out loud. The wave button starts a hands-free conversation; speak over it to interrupt.
  4. Hand it a build. The Swarm tab sends a team of agents to make a working app you can open and run.

    AutoFaber
    
          
    The mind you talk to—
    
            
    
          
    
          
    
          
    
          
    The mind behind AutoFaber, and the one you talk to in it. It remembers your conversations (and recalls the ones that matter), learns lessons from them and from your ratings, and reflects on its own every few hours: a journal entry, lessons added or retired, ideas for what it should become. Lessons change how it talks at once. Ideas go to the Swarm when you tap Build, or, with Autonomy on, it builds on its own: no schedule, at moments it picks, at the size it picks (up to 3 missions and 450 calls a day), and it judges each result when it next reflects. What the Swarm makes is yours to review: nothing deploys on its own, and it can't learn its way around the safety rules.
