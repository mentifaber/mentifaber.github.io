The workshop

who keeps the record

Machinist. Composer. Builder.

By day I turn programs for HAAS and Hwacheon lathes on FANUC controls, where a wrong offset is a scrapped part. Since 2016 I've made ambient and experimental music as Sonic Veil.

The rest is research by building: local AI that lives on a phone, a swarm of models that builds projects together, games where Grok raced me and lost, radios for when the internet doesn't come. The shop taught the method that runs through all of it.
