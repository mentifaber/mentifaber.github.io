AutoFaber, on this device

A small language model, downloaded once and run on your own device's graphics chip, in this browser tab. No server answers you, nothing you type leaves the device, and once it's downloaded it keeps working when the network doesn't. It's smaller than the online AutoFaber: good for drafting, explaining and thinking out loud, weaker on facts.

Even before it's downloaded, or on a device that can't run it, it answers from what's already on this phone: the Blackout Kit's guides, your House Manual and your street's Street Board. Ask below.

This browser can't run it.

It needs WebGPU: Chrome or Edge on a computer, Chrome on a recent Android phone, or Safari 26 on a recent iPhone, iPad or Mac. The online AutoFaber works everywhere.
