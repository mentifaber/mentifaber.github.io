# Mentifaber

**[mentifaber.org](https://mentifaber.org)**: 42 projects by Anders Powell, 30 of them running right now. AI swarms and agents, private messengers, live atlases of the sky and the world, local-first tools and games, drawn as the workshop they're built in.

This repository is the site itself: every page, the apps on it, and the Cloudflare Worker behind them (`src/`).

## Start with these

- **[AutoFaber](https://mentifaber.org/hive)**: A mind with a name that remembers you, talks with you out loud and calls on every AI model you can reach: one at a time, as a council, or as a swarm that builds whole projects.
- **[MentiPlayer](https://mentifaber.org/mentiplayer)**: Films, TV, live TV, music, radio, audiobooks and podcasts from seventeen open sources, in one player: public-domain cinema, classic TV, news channels, NASA footage and live radio, with host-hosted shows played in their own players.
- **[Phone MCP](https://mentifaber.org/phone-mcp)**: A console for any MCP server: connect to your own over Streamable HTTP, list its tools and call them with forms built from their schemas, or watch a simulated model drive a demo Android phone through ADB.
- **[Overhead](https://mentifaber.org/orbit)**: Live satellite tracking from CelesTrak element sets, propagated with SGP4 in your browser: ground tracks, the day-night line, and every pass over you, with the ones you can see by eye marked.
- **[Cartograph](https://mentifaber.org/cartograph)**: A 3D atlas from the planets to the first galaxies: 140+ objects at their real positions across ten views, each cited to the instrument that measured it, plus live satellites around Earth.
- **[Tether](https://mentifaber.org/tether)**: A one-finger rope-swinging game, open to everyone: real rope physics over lava through ten biomes with their own weather and soundtracks.
- **[NULLSPACE](https://mentifaber.org/nullspace)**: An encrypted comms deck that talks over the internet and over LoRa mesh radio (Meshtastic and MeshCore).
- **[Worm Eater](https://mentifaber.org/worm-eater)**: An ingredient scanner for food, cosmetics and personal care that says "I don't know" instead of quietly scoring the unknowns as safe.
- **[Omnilogos](https://mentifaber.org/omnilogos)**: Read any chapter of the Bible side by side in up to 22 public-domain texts, from the Hebrew, Greek and Latin originals to English and five other languages, and follow shared words and themes through a live graph.
- **[Unlit](https://mentifaber.org/conflict-atlas)**: Twenty-eight active conflicts, tiered by scale rather than by how much coverage they get.

Every other project is on the [floor plan](https://mentifaber.org).
