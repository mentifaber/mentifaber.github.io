Repair, IT, hosting, websites and software

Everything on this site was designed and built here, and is kept running here: forty-odd apps, an AI and an operating system. The same hands can fix your laptop, look after your network, host your site and build the thing you've been imagining.

Tech repair

Phones, laptops, desktops, consoles and the odd strange gadget.

  • Diagnosis first, in plain words: what's wrong, what it costs, whether it's worth it
  • Screens, batteries, ports, storage and RAM upgrades, cleaning and thermal work
  • Slow machines made fast again; malware and pop-up junk removed
  • Data rescue from failing drives and dead phones, where it's still there to rescue

Mail-in, by arrangement: no drop-off or visits for now. Anything software is fixed remotely. Every repair comes with a signed Device Passport.

IT services

The tech side of a home, a studio or a small business, handled.

  • Wi-Fi, networks, printers and shared drives set up properly, with you on the line
  • Backups that run by themselves, and a test restore so you know they work
  • Accounts, passwords and two-step sign-in sorted for the whole household or team
  • Security check-ups: what's exposed, what's out of date, what to fix first

Remote only, one-off or ongoing: you show me the setup on a call, or I connect with your permission. If a job truly needs hands on site, the quote says so first.

Web hosting

Sites kept up on the same global edge network this workshop runs on.

  • Every plan: HTTPS and a global CDN, behind Cloudflare with its standard DDoS protection
  • Every plan: uptime checked around the clock, with an alert to me when a site goes down
  • Every plan: updates applied, and nightly backups of the site's files and data
  • Your own domain, set up and renewed; email forwarding and DNS handled
  • Room to grow: forms, sign-ins, storage and APIs when the site needs them

Monthly plans, quoted to the site; your quote says how long backups are kept. Moving an existing site in is part of the job. See the hosting terms for what a restore covers.

Websites

Designed and built by hand, fast on any phone, and yours to keep.

  • Portfolios, shops, booking pages, landing pages and full business sites
  • Written to be found: search-ready pages, real descriptions, fast loads
  • Accessible, works offline where it helps, and installable as an app
  • A redesign of the site you already have, without losing what works

Fixed price per project, with the plan agreed before building starts.

Custom software & AI

The kind of thing on every bench of this workshop, built for you.

  • Web and Android apps, internal tools, dashboards and automations
  • AI assistants grounded in your own documents, with your data kept private
  • Integrations: hook the services you already pay for together
  • Prototypes in days, to find out if an idea works before it costs much

Scoped together first; built in short steps you can see and steer.

Where the work happens

Everything is remote or mail-in for now: there's no drop-off counter and no on-site visits. Software problems, IT help, hosting, websites and custom software are done remotely, wherever you are. A device that needs hands-on repair comes by mail: once you've approved the quote, you get the address and packing notes, you send it tracked and insured, and it comes back the same way. Shipping both ways is set out in the quote.

How a job runs

  1. Tell me what's going on. The form below, or an email. A photo or a link helps.
  2. A written quote. What's wrong or what you need, what it will take and the price, before anything starts.
  3. You say yes, or don't. Nothing is done or charged until you approve the quote, from your own private link in the Workshop Portal. There you can read and approve the quote, follow each step of the job, write to me, see the invoice, and open your repair's Device Passport or your hosted site's status. No account or password: the link is your key.
  4. Done and explained. You get a short write-up of what was done, and a repaired device gets a signed Device Passport.

Payments, refunds, the repair warranty, data and backups, and hosting cancellation: see the terms.
