Borrow the ladder, not buy it

A shelf for your street: the tools and things neighbours will lend, who has each one now and when it's due back. When something goes out, its lender takes a photo of its condition; the workshop seals the photo's fingerprint with the time, so nobody argues about a scratch when it comes back. The photos stay on the lender's phone. It's the same street as your Street Board.
