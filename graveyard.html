You asked me for a game, from me to you, and said you'd give me no input. So I didn't ask you anything: not the idea, the name, the look, the sound, or the login. Every choice here is mine, based on what I've seen you build and get excited about while we've worked together.

You build at night. Your site is full of orbits and signals: Vigil propagating live satellites over a globe, Cartograph's star maps, a mesh network, dashboards watching the world. You'd rather understand how a system works than be handed an answer. You wanted Flutterbloom to look like Unreal, and you lit up over ducks. And you spent a night fighting a registrar outage and a quick tunnel whose URL changed on every restart.

So I made you a job: the graveyard shift at a relay station, putting a network back into the sky one orbit at a time. It isn't a clone of anything. The physics is real, the network is real, and the music is just the network working.

It's yours. Tell me what's broken or what you'd add, and I'll handle the rest. — Claude

Why it's this game

Orbits, because VigilNothing moves on rails. Every satellite follows the orbit its launch gives it, under real inverse-square gravity. The moon tugs on anything that passes close.
A network, because MeshNetSatellites link only when they're in range and the planet isn't in the way. Every signal finds its own route through whatever you've built, hop by hop.
Night shift, because you build at 3 a.m.Ten nights, each with a log from the station, ending at dawn. Then there's an endless mode for the nights you can't sleep.
Kessler weather, because Orbit WatchDebris wrecks what it touches, and every wreck adds more debris. Fly clean, or watch your sky fill up.
A quick tunnel, because you knowA wormhole links two sides of the planet, and every time it restarts its address changes. It's a little joke about your night with the tunnels.
Sound, because SonicVeilEvery relayed signal plays a note, and longer chains climb the scale. A busy, healthy network sounds like music. Under it there's a low drone, like a server room at night.
One duckOnce a night, a rubber duck might go up as a free relay. It's the same energy as the ducks in Flutterbloom.
