The model

Attestation is not truth

A Bayesian score over cross-translation agreement. Corpus mean lands at 42.5%. It measures how strongly a verse is attested across independent transmission lines — nothing more, and the vault says so in plain language.

The shipped model uses Jaccard similarity. Stage 8 moves it to TF-IDF cosine — Jaccard lets short verses score artificially high on shared function words.
