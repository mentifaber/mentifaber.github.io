Check the quote before you pay

Paste a quote or an invoice from any shop or contractor: a car repair, a plumber, a roofer, a phone repair. Each line is set against what that part or that work typically costs, the sums are checked, and you get the questions worth asking. Every line it talks about is quoted from your document word for word.
