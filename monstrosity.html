why this exists

Opened, understood, grown

the idea

Not a rented cloud tier and not a black box you trust and forget — a stack built to be opened, understood, and grown. Storage is one organ among many. It's also where the rest of the atlas is meant to live: the log names Weaver and muse among the first tenants.

the plan
  • 01 · The private line — a gigabit switch puts all three machines on one segment; machine-to-machine traffic never touches the open internet, and static leases keep anything from drifting on reboot.
  • 02 · Fedora & the source of truth — the Fedora server anchors it; Samba pulls ten drives and three SSDs, scattered across boxes, into one shared namespace. Naming is decided before anything mounts.
  • 03 · Docker, so nothing clashes — every service in its own container: NextCloud first, then whatever Weaver and muse expose as a web UI.
  • 04 · Traefik — watches the Docker socket and routes new containers the moment their labels appear. No manual proxy edits, ever.
  • 05 · Cloudflare Tunnel — the only front door. No inbound ports on the home firewall, full stop; everything else stays LAN-bound.
the rule

Isolate the layer before you debug it. Drive, share, container, proxy, tunnel — each keeps its own logs, and the log's troubleshooting section starts from dmesg and ends at cloudflared without ever guessing across layers.
