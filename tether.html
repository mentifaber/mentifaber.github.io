What's under the hood

Real rope physicsThe rope is a distance constraint with sub-stepped integration and radial velocity removal, the same trick physics engines use. Holding reels you in and pumps the swing, so speed is earned with timing, not a button.
One input, a lot of skillHold to grab the best anchor ahead, let go to fly. If you release rising forward at speed, it's a PERFECT: points, a little extra speed, and a combo that climbs to ×12.
An endless worldThe level builds itself ahead of you from a seed. Laser gates appear around 350m, then saws, then gates that move. Six biomes cross-fade every 1,400m.
Game feelSlow-motion on near misses, screen shake, hit flashes, particle trails, haptics, and a synth soundtrack whose filter opens as you speed up. The arpeggio only plays while you're on the rope.
Your ghostYour best run is recorded 20 times a second and races you as a ghost on every new attempt. The daily run gives everyone the same world all day, with its own ghost.
Co-opTwo players, one screen. Player 1 uses the mouse (or the left half of a phone), and player 2 is green on the space bar (or the right half). Fall off the back of the screen and you're out. You have three shared revives, and when one is used you respawn on your partner's rope. If you both PERFECT within half a second, that's a HIGH FIVE.
Fair worldsEvery run starts on a rope. Anchors are always 210 to 320 apart and within reach of the last one. Laser gaps sit on the swing path, and a rope shortens itself before it can swing you into the lava.
OrbsShards buy nine orbs, including a shark one. You know why.
