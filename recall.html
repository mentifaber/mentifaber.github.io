Is anything in the house recalled?

The appliances and devices in your Home Record and House Manual, checked against the US Consumer Product Safety Commission's recalls, with the hazard and what to do, quoted from the recall. Only the make, the model number and what kind of thing it is are sent to check, never the record. Check again any time: new recalls are marked.

A recall lists the model numbers it covers, so a model number finds the most. Where a thing has none, its make and what it is are checked together, and a recall found that way says so: check its model numbers against yours.
