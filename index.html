05

Play

Games: some public, some made as gifts.

  • TetherA one-finger rope-swinging game, open to everyone: real rope physics over lava through ten biomes with their own weather and soundtracks
  • ThoughtformA first-person shooter set inside a mind under siege, built from nothing but maths: a hand-written raymarched world, live-synthesized sound and no engines, models, textures or samples
  • Watch Your GrassArena defense on a 6×6 lawn: ten plant and terrain types, six kinds of visitor, drawn up on paper before a line of code and now playable in the browser.
  • Moon CompatibilityAny two dates placed on the synodic cycle, both moons drawn and overlaid, the gap between them read as a compatibility score.
  • SketchesSmall builds, made in an afternoon and kept as sketches
  • SidewaysA drifting game Claude made for Anders where the soundtrack is played by how you drive: get sideways and an arpeggio comes in, hold it and the bass and lead follow, every orb is a note on the beat
  • Root CauseAn on-call detective game Claude made for Anders, unprompted: the pager goes off, the logs are full of red herrings, and you run real checks to find the first domino
  • Graveyard ShiftA night-shift orbit game Claude designed and built for Anders with no input from him: put relay satellites into real orbits, route signals across the network, and keep the stations talking until dawn.
  • FlutterbloomA 3D garden grown as a gift for one person
