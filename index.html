who keeps the record

Machinist. Composer. Builder.

By day, turning programs for HAAS and Hwacheon lathes on FANUC controls, where a wrong offset is a scrapped part. Since 2016, ambient and experimental music as Sonic Veil.

The rest is research: local AI that lives on a phone, a mind that persists between sessions, radios that work when the internet doesn't. The shop taught the method that runs through all of it — measure it, don't assume it.
