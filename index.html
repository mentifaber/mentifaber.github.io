Signals & Comms

Messengers, radios and things that talk without the usual pipes.

  • BarycenterA private universe for two: messages, voice notes, touch and presence, encrypted end to end and kept on your own devices, private
  • NULLSPACEAn encrypted comms deck that talks over the internet and over LoRa mesh radio (Meshtastic and MeshCore), live, installs as an app
  • PilotfishA shared list and a quiet signal line for two: what to grab, how you feel, what you need, private
  • HoplineA browser front-end for a Meshtastic LoRa mesh, live, installs as an app
  • SkiplineHF radio chat that works with the internet switched off, coming soon
  • BodywakeDetects a person walking through a house from the shadow their body casts on ambient WiFi, coming soon
  • PhosphorA remote for Roku TVs, live · Android app
  • Blackout KitGet ready while there's still a connection: mesh and radio messaging, a satellite tracker, your documents and a world map that work offline, where you are by GPS, and plain guides for water, food, heat and power., live, installs as an app
  • Street BoardA noticeboard for your street that keeps working in an outage: needs, offers, road news and who's safe, live, installs as an app
  • Street LibraryBorrow the ladder, not buy it: your street's shelf of tools and things neighbours lend each other, live, installs as an app
  • Repair CaféA pop-up repair day for your street: neighbours book a slot from a Street Board notice, the door keeps the queue with Fix or Replace's verdict on each thing, and every fix from the workshop's table leaves with a Device Passport., live
  • Sealed LettersLetters that open later: on a date, or if you stop checking in, live

AI & Minds

Swarms, agents, voices and minds that run on their own.

  • AutoFaberA mind with a name that remembers you, talks with you out loud and calls on every AI model you can reach: one at a time, as a council, or as a swarm that builds whole projects., live, installs as an app
  • WeaverA persistent, autonomous entity running locally, coming soon
  • museAn AI that writes, plays and sings its own music on its own schedule, coming soon
  • NobodyA voice assistant that runs on the phone itself, not in an app store, live, installs as an app
  • Phone MCPA console for any MCP server: connect to your own over Streamable HTTP, list its tools and call them with forms built from their schemas, or watch a simulated model drive a demo Android phone through ADB., live, installs as an app
  • MoodfieldFour small simulated networks that model shifting emotional state, with a guide to what each one does, live controls, and a straight account of what the runs do not prove., live, installs as an app
  • LexiconThe words this work needed and didn't have, from verel to mentifaber: each one defined, dated, sourced and citable., live
  • AutoFaber OfflineAutoFaber with no server at all: a small language model downloaded once and run on your own device's graphics chip, live

Sky & World

Live atlases of the planet, the sky and everything in between.

  • VigilA private command screen, private
  • OverheadLive satellite tracking from CelesTrak element sets, propagated with SGP4 in your browser: ground tracks, the day-night line, and every pass over you, with the ones you can see by eye marked., live, installs as an app
  • CartographA 3D atlas from the planets to the first galaxies: 140+ objects at their real positions across ten views, each cited to the instrument that measured it, plus live satellites around Earth., live, installs as an app
  • UnlitTwenty-eight active conflicts, tiered by scale rather than by how much coverage they get, live, installs as an app
  • Per CapitaForty-three US cities, four crime categories, FBI data projected to 2025, live, installs as an app
  • Sky LensPoint your phone at the sky and see which satellites are there: the ISS, Hubble and the bright ones, drawn over the camera where they really are, with the next ISS pass., live

Tools

Everyday instruments: money, sleep, documents, encryption.

  • BallastEvery bill on a real calendar, ninety days projected forward, and one honest number: what's safe to spend today, live, installs as an app
  • Sleep CompressionA sleep-restriction protocol turned tracker, live, installs as an app
  • CryptoVaultEncryption you can read to the bottom of: RSA-4096 and AES-256-GCM in one HTML file, with identities, contacts, signatures and files, live, installs as an app
  • Worm EaterAn ingredient scanner for food, cosmetics and personal care that says "I don't know" instead of quietly scoring the unknowns as safe, live, installs as an app
  • DocCrawlerA local document library for the phone, live, installs as an app
  • OmnilogosRead any chapter of the Bible side by side in up to 22 public-domain texts, from the Hebrew, Greek and Latin originals to English and five other languages, and follow shared words and themes through a live graph., live, installs as an app
  • Mechanic's ArsenalMachines you can take apart in the browser, live, installs as an app
  • The MonstrosityA bolted-together homelab, build log 001, coming soon
  • DuelyOverdue invoices, chased politely: three dated reminders, friendly to firm, checked against hard legal guardrails and sent from your own mail app, live, installs as an app
  • WitnessProve a photo existed by a given time and hasn't changed since, live
  • Device PassportA signed record of a repair: the work, the parts and their numbers, battery health and warranty, which travels with the device and anyone can check, including whoever buys it next., live
  • House ManualYour home's own handbook: where the shutoffs are, the Wi-Fi, bin days, each appliance with its model and warranty, and who to call, live, installs as an app
  • Fix or ReplaceA straight answer before you spend: what a repair should cost in parts and labour, what the device is worth, and whether to fix it or replace it, with the sums shown and one tap to ask the workshop for a quote., live, installs as an app
  • Care CircleLooking after someone, shared: medicines and when they're due (and who gave them), appointments, who's visiting when and the family's rota, on one page, with their Quiet Check-in at the top, live, installs as an app
  • The Home RecordA service history for the house: every appliance and device with its receipts, manuals, warranty, Device Passports, Fix or Replace checks and repairs, live, installs as an app
  • Bill WatchThis month's phone, internet, power or insurance bill against last month's: every charge that went up, every new fee and every discount that ended, quoted from the bill, with the questions to ask, live, installs as an app
  • Move-in, Move-outA rental's condition, proved twice: walk each room with photos at move-in and at move-out, each walk sealed with the date by the workshop, live, installs as an app
  • Claim KitA dispute, documented: photos, receipts and dated notes in one kit the workshop seals with its clock, and anyone can check nothing in it has changed, live, installs as an app
  • Second OpinionCheck any quote before you pay: a car repair, a plumber, a roofer, live, installs as an app
  • Digital EstateYour accounts and devices, and who should get each, sealed as a letter whose key is split among people you choose, live, installs as an app
  • Quiet Check-inFor someone who lives alone: one tap a day to say they're OK, and if it doesn't come by their time, the family's phones are told, live

Play

Games: some public, some made as gifts.

  • TetherA one-finger rope-swinging game, open to everyone: real rope physics over lava through ten biomes with their own weather and soundtracks, live, installs as an app
  • ThoughtformA first-person shooter set inside a mind under siege, built from nothing but maths: a hand-written raymarched world, live-synthesized sound and no engines, models, textures or samples, live, installs as an app
  • Watch Your GrassArena defense on a 6×6 lawn: ten plant and terrain types, six kinds of visitor, drawn up on paper before a line of code and now playable in the browser., live, installs as an app
  • SynodicAny two dates placed on the synodic cycle, both moons drawn and overlaid, the gap between them read as a compatibility score., live, installs as an app
  • SketchesSmall builds, made in an afternoon and kept as sketches, live
  • SidewaysA drifting game Claude made for Anders where the soundtrack is played by how you drive: get sideways and an arpeggio comes in, hold it and the bass and lead follow, every orb is a note on the beat, private
  • Root CauseAn on-call detective game Claude made for Anders, unprompted: the pager goes off, the logs are full of red herrings, and you run real checks to find the first domino, private
  • Graveyard ShiftA night-shift orbit game Claude designed and built for Anders with no input from him: put relay satellites into real orbits, route signals across the network, and keep the stations talking until dawn., private
  • FlutterbloomA 3D garden grown as a gift for one person, private
