Witness

Prove a photo existed by a certain time and hasn't been changed since: for a dented car before a claim, a flat at move-out, a repair before and after. The photo never leaves your phone. Only its fingerprint (a SHA-256) is sent, and the workshop signs it with its own clock.

What a record proves: this exact photo existed by the time shown, and hasn't changed by a single pixel since. It can't prove what's in the photo is real, or that the phone's reported position is true. Visitors get 100 records a day.
