How the music works

CruisingA pad holds the chord changes. Once you're moving, the kick and hats come in. Slow down and a low-pass filter closes over everything.
SidewaysStart a drift and an arpeggio comes in over the current chord.
Holding itThe multiplier climbs the longer you stay sideways. At ×2 a bassline joins, at ×4 a lead starts improvising, and at ×6 the arpeggio jumps an octave.
OrbsEvery glowing orb is a note, and it lands on the next sixteenth. You play the melody by choosing where to drive.
Four tracks, four songsGolden Hour in D Lydian, Cassette in warped A Dorian, Salt Flats in open E pentatonic, and Overdrive in fast F♯ minor.
The wagonIt's long, boxy and has roof rails. Six paint colours. It's not a sports car, and that's the joke.
