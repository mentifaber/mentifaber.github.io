Settings & help

How to use it

  • Tap any big button, or
  • tap the top bar and say what you want. It keeps listening until you say “stop”.

Things you can say

  • “Phone” — opens your phone
  • “Call 555 123 4567” — dials that number
  • “Call Mom” — calls a saved contact (add them below)
  • “Search pizza near me” — searches Google
  • “Search” — asks you what to look for
  • “Talk” — opens Talk
  • “YouTube cooking videos” or “Play some music” — searches YouTube
  • “Help” — reads this list out loud
  • “Stop” — stops listening

Calls never go through on their own: your phone shows the number first, and you press its call button.

    Privacy

    Big Buttons has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your settings and saved people stay on this device. Speech is turned into text by your browser or phone — on Chrome and Android that's Google's speech service, on iPhone it's Apple's — and only while the microphone is on.
