The dates buried in letters

Drop in the letters, renewals and notices. Every date that asks something of you comes out (pay by, renew on, reply before, an appointment, an expiry) with the sentence it's in, ready to add to your calendar as one file. Warranties from The Home Record and the next charge of each subscription Subscription Sweep found join the list. It's all worked out on this device: nothing is sent anywhere.

Coming up

The file opens in any calendar: Apple, Google, Outlook. Each date gets a reminder: three days before, or a day before an appointment. Untick any you don't want in it.

From your other apps

The list is kept on this device only.
